Process

From the first listen to the last night of the tour.

Listen

Start with the setlist. Every song gets a mood, a palette and a tempo map.

Draw

Sketches by hand and by machine. The plotter turns sound into line.

Build

Motion design, mapped to the stage, amps, screens or whatever surface is there.

Run

Programmed to cues, or performed live and synced to the band every night.

About

Artist for hire.

Michael Zimmerman builds visuals for live music: projections that wrap around gear, motion loops for LED walls, and drawings from a homemade drawing machine.

He works with bands, venues and festivals to make shows that look as good as they sound.
